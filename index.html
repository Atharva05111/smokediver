SmokeDiver

Breathing-apparatus air management, trained with your own breath.

Firefighters in smoke breathe from a cylinder that runs out. Panicked breathing empties it fast; slow, controlled breathing buys minutes. SmokeDiver reads your breathing, pulse and speech through your webcam and drains your cylinder in real time while you search a burning warehouse.

No Presage API key found. Add SMARTSPECTRA_API_KEY to the .env file, then restart. The simulator works without one.

A training game, not a medical device and not a fitness-for-duty test.

Before the camera turns on

  • What it reads. Your breathing (rate, depth, inhale and exhale timing), pulse, and whether your mouth is moving when you speak. Presage SmartSpectra measures these from the video of your face and upper chest.
  • Where video goes. Frames are processed by the SmartSpectra SDK on this computer. SmokeDiver never records or saves video or images, and never opens your microphone.
  • What leaves your computer. The SDK contacts Presage to check your API key and usage. SmokeDiver switches the SDK's optional telemetry off. While measuring, the SDK itself also sends derived numbers (pulse rate, heart-rate variability and breathing rate, never video) to Presage for its AI "insights" feature, about once a minute. SmokeDiver never requests or shows these insights and has no setting to stop them; Presage's privacy policy covers how long they are kept. Breathing-only mode below leaves out pulse and heart-rate variability.
  • What is kept. After each run, a summary (pressure, breathing rate, pulse and score over time) is saved in this app's folder on your computer, so you can compare runs. You can delete it at any time from Past runs.
  • One person only. Only point the camera at yourself. Don't use SmokeDiver to measure anyone without their knowledge.
  • Not medical. Readings are camera estimates for a game. They don't diagnose anything and don't say whether you are fit for duty.

  3. Pulse signal

Sit about an arm's length from the camera with a light in front of you. Rest your arms and keep still; the game is played with the keyboard.

Pre-entry breathing check

Breathe normally for 30 seconds, the way you would sitting in the truck on the way to a job. This sets your baseline, so the cylinder drains by how you breathe compared to your own normal.

        Privacy and data

        • Camera. Only turns on after you agree, and only while you're on the mask check, breathing check or a mission. It turns off on the debrief and when you leave. Electron is set to grant this app camera access only, never the microphone.
        • Processing. Presage's SmartSpectra SDK processes video frames on this computer. SmokeDiver keeps no video, images or face landmarks.
        • Network. The SDK contacts Presage to validate your API key, check usage and load its models. SDK telemetry is switched off (enableTelemetry: false). Separately, Presage's SDK automatically sends snapshots of derived pulse rate, heart-rate variability and breathing rate (no video or images) to Presage's AI insights service while a session runs, roughly once a minute. SmokeDiver never asks for or displays insights and cannot turn these snapshots off; see Presage's privacy policy for retention and deletion. Breathing-only mode stops pulse being measured at all.
        • Minimal camera time. The SDK is stopped as soon as a mission ends, so nothing is measured while you read the debrief or browse the board.
        • Storage. Run summaries go to runs.json in this app's data folder (on Windows, under %APPDATA%\SmokeDiver). The last 50 runs are kept. Delete them from Past runs.
        • Sharing. SmokeDiver sends nothing to anyone else.
        • Not medical. Breathing and pulse are camera estimates used to drive a game. SmokeDiver makes no health claims and does not judge fitness for duty.
